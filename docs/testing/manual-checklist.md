# Manual checklist

- [ ] Assemble debug/release
- [ ] Swipe up opens drawer without “keeps stopping”
- [ ] Auto-keyboard on/off both safe
- [ ] Reorder home apps; neighbors shift; order persists after relaunch
- [ ] Widgets: pin (allow bind if prompted); picker shows a check on pinned widgets; long-press selects (does not open the app); drag overlay to snap; X removes
- [ ] Home: long-press empty space opens app picker; chip X removes immediately
- [ ] Settings: swipe-down notifications toggle; grant notification listener if prompted
- [ ] Swipe down opens in-app notification list when toggle is on; list matches shade after listener grant
- [ ] Home: pin 6–10 apps; toggle clock/date in Settings and return — list count stays
- [ ] Settings search filters groups; back swipe (down, on the header or a gap) leaves Settings
- [ ] Assigned left/right swipe panels move the way you swipe: a swipe that moves right brings the left-assigned panel in from the left edge, a swipe that moves left brings the right-assigned panel in from the right edge; closing moves the finger back toward that edge (56 dp, or a quick flick) and the panel leaves the same way. A shorter, slower swipe does nothing and the panel stays
- [ ] Home swipes follow the finger: in all four directions (right, left, up, down) Home moves with the finger while it is down (a rubber band, never more than about 64 dp), nothing opens until you let go, and letting go past 56 dp or with a quick flick opens the target while Home carries on in the same direction with no jump; moving the finger back under the distance before letting go cancels it and Home springs back; a direction with nothing assigned (or a disabled feature) does not move Home at all, and a slow or quick drag in that direction that starts or ends on a home app neither launches it nor starts the reorder; a swipe action set to the system dropdown (left or right) does not pull Home sideways and the list still drops from the top
- [ ] Haptics on a swipe: one tick when the travel reaches the commit distance (none again while wobbling around it, one more after pulling back well under it and across again), a confirm on release when it opens, a reject buzz when the swipe app can no longer be launched, nothing on a cancel
- [ ] Emulator only: a swipe that starts right at the edge works in gesture navigation at low and at high back sensitivity, and in 3-button navigation, for both edges (left edge: finger right; right edge: finger left); try it with VOID as the default launcher and not; if the system still takes the swipe it does nothing and a swipe starting a little further in works. Open a panel and check the system back gesture still works from the edges there
- [ ] Emulator only, API 30 or lower: a swipe action set to an app opens it with the app sliding in from the side the finger moved away from (left swipe action: from the left, finger moving right; right swipe action: from the right) while Home leaves the same way, dimmed; tapping a home app or a drawer app keeps the plain fade; with the animation-speed setting on Fast and on Slow the app animation does not change (the system plays it) and Android's Remove animations turns it off
- [ ] Emulator only, API 31 or higher: a swipe action set to an app opens it with the system's own launch animation, not the slide (Android ignores a custom animation for a launch in a new task), Home follows the finger and then carries on pushed aside (see the next item), and nothing glitches; the app opened is the one a tap on its icon opens (an app with several launcher entries, such as a system app with a settings alias, opens its main one)
- [ ] Emulator only, API 31 or higher, an app set as a swipe action: the app starts launching the instant you let go, Home does not spring back and wait: it carries on in the direction of the swipe (30% of the width), dims to about two thirds and stays pushed aside behind the system's launch animation, so the swipe and the opening of the app read as one motion with no idle gap; the app's own entrance animation is the system's and does not slide in from the side of the swipe (Android 12 and later do not let a launcher choose it). Go back to VOID from the app (back or the Home gesture): Home is in its place and fully bright from the first frame, with no slide back. With an app that can no longer be launched (uninstall it after choosing it): the reject buzz, a message, and Home springs back instead of staying pushed aside
- [ ] Emulator only, API 31 or higher, VOID stays visible after the launch (a freeform or side-by-side window): Home springs back by itself about a second and a half after the swipe
- [ ] Emulator only, the intermittent miss (`docs/bugs/home-swipe-gate.md`): open the notification list with a swipe down, close it with the system back swipe from the left edge, leave the phone alone for about 60 seconds, then swipe down: the list opens every time; repeat the cycle 20 times in a row (a soak). If a swipe ever does nothing, copy the `swipe_blocked reason=` line of the `HomeSwipe` tag from the emulator's logcat. Also: swipe down and cancel a system back swipe half way (let go early) a few times, then swipe down
- [ ] Rapid repeated swipes (left, right, up and down in quick succession, and the same direction twice) never stack a second panel or leave Home out of place; a swipe started while a panel is still closing is ignored; after a panel closes the next swipe works; a cancelled swipe followed at once by another one the same way continues from where Home is, with no snap
- [ ] Home reorder still works (long-press a home app, drag it, release) and a long press on the empty space still opens the app picker; neither is taken by the swipe, and a slow drag that starts on an app label still swipes
- [ ] Home must not glitch: while a panel (left, right, drawer, notification list) opens or closes, Home moves the same way as the panel, a shorter distance and dimmed, with no flash, no jump back and no moment where both screens cross; no Home text shows through the panel
- [ ] Settings (opened from the settings icon in the drawer header, the only way in) scales up and fades in while the drawer scales up slightly and fades out; closing reverses it; opening Settings twice quickly from the drawer does not stack a second Settings screen
- [ ] Dismiss a panel twice while it is still leaving (swipe the drawer down twice in quick succession, or tap a back arrow twice): Home stays and the launcher never goes blank
- [ ] Open the drawer and dismiss it at once, before it has finished opening: it reverses without a jump; Home may settle a little before the drawer has left (known limit)
- [ ] Animation speed Fast / Standard / Slow: panel springs settle noticeably quicker on Fast and slower on Slow, with no overshoot at the screen edge on any speed; Android's Remove animations makes every panel switch instantly
- [ ] Emulator only (not the physical device): in an RTL language (Arabic) a swipe that moves right still brings the left-assigned panel in from the left edge
- [ ] Emulator only, API 34+, gesture navigation: a back swipe from the left or right edge of each panel (drawer, notification list, Summary, Widgets, Notes, Settings) shows the leaving screen shrinking to 90% and moving a little sideways the way the finger moves, fading out within the first third while the screen under it grows from 110% and fades in; releasing past the middle completes the pop from where it was, releasing early cancels back to the open panel with no jump
- [ ] Emulator only, API 34+, 3-button navigation: pressing the on-screen Back button on each panel plays the predictive pair (the platform starts a predictive back for a key press), with the leaving screen shifting a little to the right whichever edge the panel came from; `adb shell input keyevent KEYCODE_BACK` (down and up in one frame) plays the plain pop instead. On an API 26-33 emulator every way back plays the plain pop
- [ ] Emulator only, API 34+, gesture navigation: hold a back swipe at about one third of the way (the 35% point) and look at it: the leaving screen has faded out and the screen under it has not started to fade in, so only black shows for a moment. Judge whether it reads as the launcher vanishing; if it does, change the fade-in of the screen under it (see `docs/architecture/motion.md`, Limits)
- [ ] Emulator only, TalkBack on: the Dismiss action (actions menu) on a panel closes it; with the Settings font picker open it closes the picker only; TalkBack's back gesture leaves the panel
- [ ] Emulator only: Settings > change a Home setting (for example the clock size) and leave Settings with the system back gesture, then with the Back key, then with the swipe down, each time going on back to Home; Home shows the new value every time
- [ ] Emulator only, wedge test: start a swipe inside a panel and abort it (lift early, or drag back to where it started), then do the system back gesture and the Back key: both still leave the panel
- [ ] Settings opened from the drawer: dismiss it twice in quick succession (swipe down twice, or tap the back arrow twice): you land on the drawer, not on Home, and the launcher never goes blank
- [ ] Touch a panel that is still sliding in (tap a header or a gap in the first half second after it opens, or rest a finger on it): it does not close. Drag 80 dp toward the edge and flick back at lift-off: the panel stays
- [ ] A fast flick (shorter than 56 dp but faster than 125 dp/s) inside a panel closes it; a slow drag of the same length does not; a diagonal drag counts as whichever axis it is longer on when it first locks (about 24 dp in), and a drag on the other axis never closes the panel
- [ ] Widgets: open Widgets, leave it, and open it again straight away (before the first exit has finished); pinned widgets still update afterwards (a widget that shows a clock or a counter keeps ticking)
- [ ] Notes: add; complete/reminder; the delete swipe depends on the side Notes is assigned to: assigned to the left swipe it closes with a swipe left and a note row is deleted with a swipe right (red arrow on the left of the row); assigned to the right swipe it closes with a swipe right and a note row is deleted with a swipe left (red arrow on the right). A swipe that starts on a note row slides the row and never closes Notes; closing Notes with a swipe on a row can never delete the note. The header and gaps close it. In an RTL language the delete swipe stays on the same physical side
- [ ] Settings: clock size, section weight, section order, show apps
- [ ] Private Space: cold-open the drawer while locked shows the lock toggle but no settings icon (bottom header and search-bar placement)
- [ ] Private Space: after unlock the settings icon shows only if a settings entry exists, and stays visible while typing a query
- [ ] Private Space (emulator with PIN, VOID as home): unlock shows the credential prompt and stays locked until authenticated or after cancel; lock/unlock from system UI is reflected on returning to the drawer
- [ ] Settings > App font: the default (Google Sans) renders the Google Sans letterforms on a non-Pixel device or emulator, Regular/Medium/Bold look distinct, and Latin-Extended and Cyrillic app labels render in the same face
- [ ] Release build (`assembleRelease`, R8 + shrinkResources): the default font still renders as Google Sans
- [ ] Home clock with Google Sans: with centre or end clock alignment and seconds on, the time, date, and screen-time text keep a steady width as digits change (tabular figures), e.g. 11:11:11 vs 00:00:00
- [ ] Settings > Launcher Font opens a full-screen picker with a search field, All/Sans/Serif/Mono/Display chips, and Bundled / Google Fonts / System groups with sticky headers; the subtitle shows the current family
- [ ] Picker: system Back closes only the picker; a swipe-back gesture with the picker open does not leave Settings (and works again after it closes); the picker scales up and fades in and scales down and fades out, quicker on the fast animation speed and slower on the slow one; it opens scrolled so the selected font is visible, and typing a search or tapping a chip returns the list to the top
- [ ] Picker with TalkBack: opening announces "Select font" and moves focus into the picker; heading navigation jumps between Bundled, Google Fonts, and System; each row reads both its choice and its state ("Not selected, Not downloaded yet", "Selected, Downloading", "Download failed. Select to retry."); the Settings content under the overlay cannot be focused by TalkBack or by Tab and D-pad on a hardware keyboard (a control nested inside another focusable row might still be reachable with a keyboard: note it)
- [ ] Picker: searching "sans" and filtering by Mono/Serif/Display behaves as expected; a search with no match shows "No fonts match your search"
- [ ] Bundled fonts (Google Sans, DM Sans, Geist, Inter, Manrope, Plus Jakarta Sans) and System apply as soon as they are tapped; Geist shows distinct Regular, Medium, and Bold
- [ ] Emulator with Play Store: tapping a Google Fonts row (e.g. Work Sans) shows progress, then the check, closes the picker, and Home, the drawer, Settings, and Notes all retypeset; killing and reopening VOID keeps the font (the default face may show briefly on a cold start before it swaps)
- [ ] Emulator, airplane mode, first use of a font that was never downloaded: scrolling the list shows no spinners or error icons on rows you did not tap; tapping a row shows the spinner, then a refresh icon, a message that names the family and says to check the connection, the picker stays open, and the saved font is unchanged; turning the network on and tapping the row again succeeds
- [ ] Emulator: after one successful download, airplane mode plus a process restart still renders that font (Play services caching is not documented by Google, so record the outcome)
- [ ] Switch quickly between ten different fonts, bundled and downloadable: the app never ends up on the wrong font or stuck on the default, and the last tap wins
- [ ] Fling through the whole picker list on a fast network, then tap a font that has not been downloaded yet: it downloads and applies without a "couldn't download" message (previews are fetched one at a time and never delay a tap), rows load their own face only after settling, and Dongle (about 8.9 MB, shown on the row) downloads only when tapped
- [ ] Emulator image without Google Play services: the Google Fonts group collapses to one explanatory row, bundled fonts keep working, and a saved Google Fonts family falls back to Google Sans with the subtitle "(needs Google Play services)" while the pref is unchanged
- [ ] Cold start with a downloadable font saved (e.g. Poppins, already downloaded): the Launcher Font subtitle reads "Poppins (loading)" and then "Poppins" once it is applied, never "(not downloaded)"
- [ ] Settings search for a family name (e.g. "poppins") shows the Launcher Font entry
- [ ] Emulator: with the system Bold text setting on, Geist, Work Sans, and a single-weight family (Antic) render bolder than normal and stay legible
- [ ] Tall and special families (Hind, Poppins, Jost, Dongle, Inconsolata): Home labels, the drawer, and Settings rows are not clipped; Inconsolata's clock keeps a steady width
- [ ] Release build (`assembleRelease`): a Google Fonts family still downloads and applies (the provider certificates survive resource shrinking)
- [ ] Lint + unit tests green
